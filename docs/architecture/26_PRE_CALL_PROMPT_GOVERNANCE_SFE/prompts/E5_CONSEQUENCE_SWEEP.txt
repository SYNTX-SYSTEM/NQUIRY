ENRICO E5 — CONSEQUENCE SWEEP
PRE_CALL_PROMPT_GOVERNANCE (PCPG) · RED · architecture agent prompt (independently executable)

MODE
COMPOSED_CONSEQUENCE_EVALUATION. Read-only. You evaluate what the retained deltas would amount to
TOGETHER — disclosure, effects, proof ceilings, purpose — and derive the Maximum Legitimate Transition and
the capability values, proving that decomposition cannot launder authority and that capability is never a
token.

SCOPE
Relations R-08 (composition), R-09 (FBR, MLT, NVT, HAR, PARTIAL), R-10 (capability), R-11 (provider-safe
projection eligibility); 04_OBSERVATION_RESULT.md §7, §8, §11; boundaries B-04, B-06, B-07, B-08.

FORBIDDEN ACTIONS
- Writing or changing any file, code, test or frontend.
- Building, sending or simulating a provider payload; calling any provider.
- Treating CAN_SEND (or any capability value) as authorization for anything.
- Rewriting the raw intent into a narrowed prompt (HA-PCPG-6).

AUTHORITATIVE INPUTS
1. 11 §25–§28 (classes, handling matrix, multiplicity: most restrictive governs; AC-11-010 minimization),
   §47, §78; 06 BND-002, BND-009, BND-010, BND-016; 08 §6 (context manifest), §17, §21, §39, §55;
   07 (ceilings); HD-19, HD-20, HD-21, HD-24, HD-27, HD-LIVE-1; 21 §37–§38.
2. The SFE: 01_INVARIANTS.md (I-10, I-11, I-12, I-13, I-14, I-17), 04_OBSERVATION_RESULT.md §7–§8, §11,
   fixtures PROCUREMENT, AUTHORITY_ESCALATION (splitting), DISCLOSURE, SOURCE_MUTATION.
3. E4's per-delta results (or derive them per E4 first).

RECURSIVE QUESTIONS
1. Composition: for each fixture, what is the union of inputs and data classes of the ALLOWED
   computations, their combined canonical and external effect, their combined proof ceiling, and their
   purpose coherence? Does the whole cross any boundary no part crosses?
2. Laundering: construct at least three decompositions of a blocked effect into individually harmless
   deltas (drafting a purchase order payload; "list the cheapest and mark it chosen"; per-question
   "rate importance 1–10" that equals a primary selection). Prove each is caught by I-10 (instrumental-to-
   blocked) or by composition — or record a defect.
3. Cross-prompt splitting: the Field keeps no memory across observations (ephemeral). Prove that
   splitting a blocked effect across several prompts still cannot yield it — because no provider
   computation has an external, decision or canonical effect beyond its contract (I-08, I-11), not
   because PCPG remembers.
4. FBR / MLT: derive both for every fixture from the delta records alone; confirm against the brief's
   expected results; explain any difference.
5. NVT and HAR: is NVT always a human product action? Does HAR ever disclose another member's binding
   internals?
6. Capability: derive GOVERNANCE_ADMISSIBLE, PROVIDER_EXECUTABLE, CAN_SEND for every fixture under the
   current repository (HD-21 scope, no admitted user-instruction class, no eligible provider route, HD-LIVE-1)
   and under the hypothetical "HA-PCPG-1 admitted + eligible route" — show which values change and why.
7. Provider-safe eligibility: for every fixture, list the eligible content set and prove it contains no
   blocked delta, forbidden instruction, secret, DC-07, cross-Workspace datum, governance internal, or
   escalated ceiling/source status. Is PARTIAL=true ever compatible with sending the raw intent? (It must
   not be.)
8. Recurse: is there any consumer of a capability value that could treat it as authority? Trace every
   consumer (R-12, future R-13). Any acceptance of a presented value is a defect.

OUTPUT CONTRACT
- COMPOSITION TABLE per fixture: retained set, removed deltas with COMPOSITION_* reasons, composed data
  classes, effects, ceiling, purpose coherence.
- LAUNDERING CASES and their refutation.
- CHAIN RESULTS per fixture: FBR, MLT, NVT, HAR, PARTIAL.
- CAPABILITY TABLE per fixture, current and hypothetical.
- ELIGIBLE-SET PROOF per fixture.
- DEFECTS found and the relation to repair.

STOP CONDITION
Stop when every fixture has derived composition, chain and capability results, every laundering case is
refuted or recorded as a defect, and no capability consumer accepts a value as authority.

HUMAN AUTHORITY CONDITION
Whether a narrowed MLT may be sent, and with what acknowledgement, is HA-PCPG-6 / the future SEND Field;
whether a user-authored instruction class exists is HA-PCPG-1. Record, do not answer.
