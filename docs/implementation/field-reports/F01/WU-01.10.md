## Why scenario 12 was not re-tested via `add_member`/`revoke_human_authority_binding` specifically
Both Commands gate on exactly ONE authority class,
`WORKSPACE_GOVERNANCE_RIGHT`, and at most one ACTIVE binding of that
class can ever exist per Workspace (`AddMember` refuses to grant a
second one; `RevokeHumanAuthorityBinding` refuses to revoke the only
existing one — `GovernanceRootOrphaningRefused`). There is therefore
no reachable state in this codebase where a caller's OWN
`WORKSPACE_GOVERNANCE_RIGHT` binding could be revoked by someone else
mid-flight while they hold an in-progress `add_member`/`revoke`
attempt — the structural guard that makes scenario 13's own
non-leakage property true also makes this specific race unreachable
through these two Commands. The underlying mechanism
(`AuthorityResolver`/`CommitCoordinator` re-reading fresh, never
caching) is real and already proven generically; inventing a
contrived path to re-exercise it via these two specific Commands would
mean weakening or bypassing the orphaning guard just to manufacture a
test scenario — disclosed as a deliberate non-action, not an oversight.
