## 2. Human Review Reconstruction

The SF-02 materialization passed functional and architectural review strongly.

The following observations are not a rejection of the current architecture.

They are the next legitimate architectural delta produced by direct use of the system.

### 2.1 Observation A: The Field needs a stronger ambient presence

The existing background is dark, calm and spatial.

It already supports the Field.

The next version should feel more alive across the complete viewport.

The human should perceive subtle continuous life even in areas with no active node or instrument.

The target is not particle spectacle.

The target is a barely perceptible atmospheric Field.

Suitable visual families include:

```text
very slow nebular drift
soft volumetric haze
low-frequency luminance movement
subtle spatial diffusion
weak field currents
slow depth gradients
rare low-energy particles
ambient orbital traces
```

Implementation may choose the concrete technique.

The architectural requirement is the effect:

```text
THE FIELD MUST FEEL ALIVE
WITHOUT DEMANDING ATTENTION.
```

---

### 2.2 Observation B: Typography and containment need stronger invariants

Some node labels and dense textual regions are visually too small or too constrained.

Text must become more readable everywhere.

Text must never escape its semantic frame.

The frame must adapt to content where legitimate.

Content must not be forced through fixed geometry that destroys readability.

The required relation is:

```text
CONTENT
→ MEASURED LEGIBILITY NEED
→ FRAME GEOMETRY
→ NODE GEOMETRY
→ ORBIT GEOMETRY
```

Not:

```text
FIXED FRAME
→ FORCE CONTENT INSIDE
```

---

### 2.3 Observation C: Orbit and node distribution should be more dynamic

The current orbital architecture is correct.

Its spatial allocation is still more regular and static than the desired final symbiotic experience.

All primary relational surfaces should use more content-aware, viewport-aware and topology-aware distribution.

The layout must feel composed by the actual Field, not positioned by a reusable diagram template.

---

### 2.4 Observation D: Breadcrumb navigation remains visually separate from the Field

The current breadcrumb works.

It does not yet feel sufficiently symbiotic.

The navigation path should become a calm orientation rail belonging to the same visual universe as the relational Field.

Its characters or segments may breathe very slowly.

This motion must remain below the threshold of active attention.

Navigation must remain highly readable.

---

### 2.5 Observation E: The right information region remains too vertically panel-like

The current right-hand instrument region is semantically correct.

Its composition still resembles a vertical application sidebar.

This creates unnecessary vertical scrolling and visually separates governance, proof, action and context from the relational Field.

The next architecture must transform this into a symbiotic information constellation.

On wide surfaces, information should use available horizontal and two-dimensional space before falling back to long vertical stacking.

---

### 2.6 Observation F: The complete Field should use more of the available spatial canvas

Primary relational surfaces should occupy the available viewport more confidently.

Nodes should have more breathing room.

Relations should have longer spatial expression where this improves comprehension.

The center must remain coherent without becoming cramped.

---

### 2.7 Observation G: NQIRY identity should move into the visual center of the global frame

The NQIRY identity currently sits as a conventional left-aligned product label.

The next architecture should give the product identity a stronger central position.

The global identity mark or wordmark should become a visually intentional center point in the top orientation layer.

This must not turn the logo into domain authority.

Product identity is presentation identity only.

## 3. Protected Architecture

The following laws remain unchanged.

### 3.1 Frontend Law

```text
FRONTEND PROJECTS SYSTEM STATE.

FRONTEND DOES NOT CREATE SYSTEM TRUTH.
```

No visual improvement may weaken this law.

---

### 3.2 Effect Law

```text
CANDIDATE != EFFECT

REQUEST != COMMIT

ANIMATION != STATE
```

No visual effect may imply success before commit.

No ambient motion may imply semantic state unless that semantic relation is explicitly established.

---

### 3.3 Authority Law

```text
IDENTITY != ROLE

ROLE != AUTHORITY

AUTHORITY != PARTICIPATION

CONTROL AUTHORITY != CONTENT VISIBILITY
```

Visual composition must never flatten these relations into one hierarchy.

---

### 3.4 Human Question Law

Human Question Capture remains active while:

```text
Session = QUESTION_GENERATION

and

Burst = ACTIVE HUMAN_ONLY
```

Closing Question Generation freezes the human question membership and transitions the Session to `QUESTION_CAPTURE`.

The visual evolution must not move question capture into `QUESTION_CAPTURE`.

---

### 3.5 Responsive Capability Law

Responsive transformation may change:

```text
geometry
density
ordering where meaning remains preserved
motion
presentation
instrument arrangement
disclosure depth
```

Responsive transformation may never silently change:

```text
system possibility
authority
capability
proof
governance
question visibility
effect reachability
semantic meaning
```

---

### 3.6 Mock Law

No visual evolution may replace an existing real relation with a mock because the real relation is harder to integrate.

Mocks remain subject to all ceilings established by document 22.

## 4. Symbiotic Coherence Law

Document 23 introduces the following global law:

```text
EVERY PRIMARY SURFACE MUST FEEL LIKE ONE FIELD.

NO PRIMARY SURFACE MAY FEEL LIKE
A FIELD ON THE LEFT
PLUS
AN APPLICATION SIDEBAR ON THE RIGHT
PLUS
A NAVIGATION BAR ABOVE IT.
```

This does not mean every region must look identical.

It means every region must visibly belong to the same relational environment.

The implementation must create continuity through:

```text
shared depth
shared spatial rhythm
shared typography
shared border language
shared motion language
shared glow discipline
shared relational highlighting
shared field background
shared geometry
shared focus behavior
```

The interface may still contain forms, panels, proof areas and control instruments.

They must feel embedded into the Field.

They may not appear pasted beside it.

## 5. Ambient Field Architecture

### 5.1 Global Living Background

Every primary frontend surface receives a global ambient Field layer.

This layer should span substantially the whole visible viewport.

It should not stop at the orbital region.

It should continue behind navigation, relational surfaces and instrument regions.

The background should provide subtle spatial continuity across the complete screen.

---

### 5.2 Nebular Motion

The background may contain a restrained moving nebular layer.

The implementation agent may choose:

```text
CSS gradients
SVG filters
canvas
GPU-composited layers
pseudo-elements
or another efficient presentation mechanism
```

The architecture does not prescribe technology.

The required visible effect is:

```text
extremely slow
low amplitude
low contrast
non-distracting
continuous
spatial
calm
organic
nervous-system friendly
```

The user should usually notice the effect more through peripheral perception than through direct attention.

---

### 5.3 Background Motion Is Presentation State

The nebular field is not semantic system state.

Its movement may not indicate:

```text
authority
commit
success
failure
loading
denial
participation
governance
system health
```

unless a separate explicit semantic motion rule establishes that meaning.

Ambient life is not authority.

---

### 5.4 Reduced Motion

Reduced-motion mode must preserve Field depth without requiring motion.

Suitable equivalents include:

```text
static diffusion
depth gradients
fixed nebular texture
soft spatial luminance
static orbital traces
```

Reduced motion must never produce a visually dead or broken surface.

---

### 5.5 Performance Boundary

The living background must remain subordinate to application responsiveness.

If its visual cost threatens interaction quality, implementation must reduce:

```text
frequency
layer count
blur complexity
particle density
filter cost
paint area
```

before reducing semantic UI quality.

The agent must empirically test the background cost.

## 6. Typography and Semantic Containment Law

### 6.1 Legibility Is an Architectural Invariant

Every material label must be readable.

Especially:

```text
Core labels
Node labels
authority labels
state names
relation state
participant identity
governance identity
proof labels
breadcrumb text
boundary explanation
action text
```

The implementation must not preserve a fixed geometry at the expense of legibility.

---

### 6.2 Text Must Never Escape Its Frame

The following is forbidden:

```text
text crossing node borders
text crossing panel borders
text covering relation paths
text colliding with adjacent nodes
text clipping without an intentional disclosure mechanism
text becoming unreadably small to preserve layout
```

This is a hard architecture falsifier.

---

### 6.3 Frames Follow Content

Where a node contains meaningful content, its geometry must be able to respond to:

```text
label length
state label length
role label length
authority label length
viewport size
font metrics
content density
```

The preferred law is:

```text
SEMANTIC CONTENT
→ LEGIBLE TYPE
→ REQUIRED FRAME
→ REQUIRED SPATIAL ALLOCATION
```

---

### 6.4 Long Technical Identifiers

Proof identifiers, UUIDs, command identifiers and authority sources may use specialized wrapping or disclosure.

They may not destroy layout.

They may not be silently removed where proof requires them.

---

### 6.5 Typography Hierarchy

The Field should use stronger visible hierarchy between:

```text
context
entity
state
relation
authority
human position
proof detail
technical identifier
```

The most important semantic layer must remain immediately readable without zooming into the interface.

## 7. Dynamic Field Geometry

### 7.1 Geometry Must Be Content-Aware

Primary Field geometry must no longer behave as though every node occupies the same semantic or physical volume.

Node placement should account for actual rendered dimensions and topology.

---

### 7.2 Geometry Must Be Viewport-Aware

The same relation graph may produce different legitimate geometries at different viewport dimensions.

The geometry may transform.

The system meaning may not.

---

### 7.3 Geometry Must Be Topology-Aware

A node should be positioned from its actual relation to the Core and neighboring relations.

The implementation may use:

```text
adaptive rings
elliptical orbits
offset rings
radial sectors
weighted angular distribution
content-aware spacing
dynamic radius
constraint solving
collision avoidance
hybrid orbital layouts
```

The architecture does not prescribe the algorithm.

---

### 7.4 Determinism

For the same:

```text
canonical state
human position
viewport class
accessibility mode
```

the resulting layout should remain stable enough that the interface does not visually reshuffle without cause.

Dynamic does not mean random.

---

### 7.5 Collision Avoidance

The geometry must prevent:

```text
node overlap
text overlap
frame overlap
unintended same-spoke stacking
relation path obstruction where avoidable
node clipping
core collision
instrument collision
```

---

### 7.6 Spatial Breathing Room

Primary Field surfaces should make better use of available space.

The Core does not need to remain compressed near surrounding nodes.

Where space exists, orbit radius and relation distance should expand.

Where space is constrained, representation may compact while preserving meaning.

---

### 7.7 No False Semantic Gravity

More physical distance does not automatically mean less importance.

Closer proximity does not automatically mean greater authority.

The layout must not accidentally invent semantic hierarchy through geometry.

---

## 8. Relational Reciprocity

Document 23 introduces a stronger symbiotic interaction behavior.

When a user hovers, focuses or otherwise selects a visible relation, related visible instruments may respond together.

Example:

```text
NODE FOCUS
→ related path becomes clearer
→ related instrument region softly responds
→ relevant proof/control area becomes easier to identify
```

And inversely:

```text
INSTRUMENT FOCUS
→ corresponding Field relation becomes easier to identify
```

This is presentation-only reciprocity.

It may never imply:

```text
new authority
new capability
new state
commit
selection persistence
```

unless the underlying system actually establishes those relations.

The purpose is to make the interface feel like one organism rather than separate widgets.

---

### 9.1 Breadcrumb Becomes an Orientation Relation

The breadcrumb must stop feeling like a conventional website breadcrumb placed above the Field.

It should become the visible orientation path through:

```text
Workspace
→ Challenge
→ Session
→ Current State
```

where applicable.

---

### 9.2 Symbiotic Motion

Breadcrumb characters, separators or segments may exhibit extremely subtle breathing.

This must not involve distracting translation or jitter.

Preferred motion families include:

```text
tiny luminance shift
tiny glow modulation
very slow opacity modulation
very slow border or separator energy
```

Text position should remain stable.

Readability is dominant.

---

### 9.3 Current Position

The current orientation point must remain immediately clear.

Historical parents should remain accessible without visually competing with the current Field.

---

### 9.4 Breadcrumb and Field Reciprocity

Where useful, breadcrumb focus may softly highlight the corresponding Field layer.

Example:

```text
Workspace breadcrumb focus
→ Workspace context subtly becomes visible

Challenge breadcrumb focus
→ Challenge relation subtly responds
```

This interaction is optional if implementation can derive it cleanly without semantic ambiguity.

The logo should feel designed, not merely typed.

---

### 10.2 Identity Does Not Become Authority

It is not:


The top orientation region should balance:

```text
navigation context
centered NQIRY identity
session/account action such as logout
```

without allowing one region to push the true visual center away from the viewport center.

---

### 10.4 Responsive Identity

On narrow screens, the identity may transform into a compact centered mark or dedicated row.

It may not cause breadcrumb overflow or hide navigation.

```text

```

is under-responsive.

is not dynamic enough.

FIRST BROKEN RELATION:

```text

```

is visually discontinuous.

Transform breadcrumb into a symbiotic orientation rail.

---

### FBR-23-05: Instrument Waterfall

FIRST BROKEN RELATION:


FIRST BROKEN RELATION:


### FBR-23-07: Weak Global Identity Center

VISIBLE SYMPTOM:

NQIRY identity appears as a conventional left-aligned application label.

FIRST BROKEN RELATION:

```text
PRODUCT IDENTITY
→ GLOBAL ORIENTATION FIELD
```

is visually underdeveloped.

Create a centered and intentional global NQIRY identity position.
